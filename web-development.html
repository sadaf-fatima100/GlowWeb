09 / Frequently Asked

Web Development FAQs

Common questions about our engineering process, tech stack, and timelines, with clear details on our dedicated post-launch support. Common questions about engineering, stack, and timelines, plus dedicated support.

What tech stack do you use for custom web development?

We architect modern platforms using modern standards — primarily Next.js, React, TypeScript, and clean semantic HTML5/CSS3. For backends, we integrate Node.js, headless CMS options, and cloud serverless architectures to guarantee 99.9% uptime and sub-second load speeds.

How long does an end-to-end website project take?

A standard custom website takes 1 to 2 weeks for our Starter tier, 2 to 3 weeks for our Professional tier, and 3 to 5 weeks for complex custom web portals or enterprise applications.

Will my website be mobile responsive and Core Web Vitals optimized?

Absolutely. Every platform we build is engineered mobile-first with adaptive layouts across smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide displays. We guarantee 90+ PageSpeed scores and green Core Web Vitals.

Do you provide post-launch maintenance, hosting support, and security updates?

Yes. All of our packages include complimentary post-launch support (from 7 to 30 days depending on the tier). We also offer monthly retainer plans for continuous security updates, CI/CD deployment, feature additions, and cloud server maintenance.

Can you migrate our existing content and preserve SEO keyword rankings?

Yes. We perform complete URL structure mapping, 301-redirect setups, schema preservation, and content parity audits before launching so your existing Google keyword rankings and organic traffic remain 100% intact.

How do payments and project milestones work?

We work with clear, transparent milestone pricing. Typically, projects are structured with a 50% initial deposit upon kickoff and 50% upon final client sign-off and deployment.
