09 / Frequently Asked

Google Ads Management FAQs

Everything you need to know about our account auditing, Smart Bidding management, attribution tracking, and ROAS guarantees. Answers on auditing, bidding and ROAS, attribution and guarantees.

How quickly can a new Google Ads campaign go live?

Following account auditing, negative keyword research, conversion tracking setup, and ad copywriting, new campaigns typically launch within 4 to 7 business days.

What monthly ad spend do you typically manage?

We manage accounts ranging from $2,000/month for growing startups up to $200,000+/month for enterprise brands across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, and Performance Max.

Do you work directly inside our own Google Ads account?

Yes. You retain 100% ownership of your Google Ads account, billing profile, and historical data at all times. We access your account via our Google MCC partner manager.

How do you prevent wasted ad spend on irrelevant search terms?

We implement extensive negative keyword libraries, sculpt match types (Exact and Phrase match priority), use device and location bid exclusions, and perform weekly search query audits.

Can you help optimize our landing pages for higher Quality Scores?

Yes. Every PPC package includes dedicated landing page conversion rate optimization (CRO) audits to align search intent, speed, and CTA hierarchy.
